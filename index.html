About Me

I'm Mohamed Farag, an AI Engineer focused on building practical AI systems powered by Generative AI, Large Language Models, RAG, and AI Agents.

I graduated from the Faculty of Artificial Intelligence at Delta University for Science and Technology, where I developed a strong foundation in AI and software development. Since then, I've focused on turning AI concepts into real, usable applications — from AI meeting assistants and intelligent career platforms to AI-powered decision systems and Arabic/English document processing.

My work combines Python, FastAPI, LLMs, RAG, AI agents, APIs, and modern AI tools to build systems that go beyond simple chatbots. I care about designing the complete pipeline: understanding the problem, choosing the right AI approach, connecting models with tools and data, building reliable APIs, and delivering an application that actually solves a problem.

I'm particularly interested in Agentic AI, Generative AI, RAG, AI Automation, and intelligent backend systems, and I'm continuously building and experimenting with new ways to make AI applications more useful, reliable, and practical.
